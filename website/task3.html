TASK 3

Do Superhosts hosts outperform regular hosts?

Comparing host performance using reviews, listing count, and revenue...

Design: This scatterplot compares total listing counts versus review scores for Airbnb listings in Boston. Each point represents a single host, with:

  • Color representing whether the listing is hosted by a professional host (consistent positive ratings and identified by AirBnb with a badge) or a regular host (not distinguished by Airbnb).
  • Point size representing the last 365 days revenue garnered by that host.
  • Hover tooltip showing review score, revenue, superhost status, and total listings.

Takeaways:

  • Professional hosts tend to generate more revenue: Listings belonging to Superhosts show toward the higher-review area of the plot, suggesting they price and operate their listings more effectively, leading to happier customers.
  • Regular hosts show more variability: Many non-Super hosts have strong reviews but significantly lower estimated revenue, indicating inconsistent performance.
  • Superhosts outperform comparable non-superhosts: Across both groups, superhosts show noticeably higher review scores and moderately higher revenue.
  • Large host portfolios do not correlate with higher revenue: The host with the largest amount of listings (shown on the right part of the chart) have comparably smaller dot sizes than the hosts on the left.
