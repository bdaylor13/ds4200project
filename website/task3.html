TASK 3

Do professional hosts outperform individual hosts?

Comparing host performance using reviews, listing count, and revenue...

Design: This scatterplot compares estimated revenue versus review scores for Airbnb listings in Boston. Each point represents a single listing, with:

  • Color representing whether the listing is hosted by a professional host (multiple listings) or an individual host (single listing).
  • Point size representing the number of listings owned by that host.
  • Hover tooltip showing host name, review score, revenue, superhost status, and total listings.

Takeaways:

  • Professional hosts tend to generate more revenue: Listings belonging to multi-property hosts cluster toward the higher-revenue area of the plot, suggesting they price and operate their listings more effectively.
  • Individual hosts show more variability: Many single-property hosts have strong reviews but significantly lower estimated revenue, indicating inconsistent performance.
  • Superhosts outperform comparable non-superhosts: Across both groups, superhosts show noticeably higher review scores and moderately higher revenue.
  • Large host portfolios correlate with higher revenue: The largest points (representing hosts with many listings) tend to appear in the upper portion of the chart, reflecting economies of scale and experience.
